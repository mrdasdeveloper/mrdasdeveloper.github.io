Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before reaching out

How quickly do you respond to inquiries?

I typically respond within 2–4 hours on WhatsApp during my working hours (Nepal time, UTC+5:45). Email responses are within 24 hours. I check messages in the evening for clients in US and Australia time zones.

Are you available for full-time remote employment?

Yes. I'm open to the right full-time remote role with a strong AI-first team. I'm also available for long-term contract or retainer arrangements that function similarly. Please share your role details via WhatsApp or email.

What is the best way to start a project with you?

The fastest way is a WhatsApp message with your project description, requirements, timeline, and budget. I'll review and schedule a short discovery call (15–30 mins) within 48 hours to discuss scope and fit before any commitment.

Do you take on small tasks or short consulting sessions?

Yes. Hourly consulting sessions are available with a minimum of 4 hours. This is ideal for architecture reviews, code audits, AI integration strategy, RAG system design, or prompt engineering optimization.

What information should I include in my first message?

To get a faster, more accurate response, include: (1) what you're building or the problem to solve, (2) your current tech stack, (3) your timeline, (4) your approximate budget or rate expectations, and (5) your preferred engagement type (hourly, retainer, or project).
