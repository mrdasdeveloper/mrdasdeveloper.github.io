AI development services & AI agent development

AI development services cover everything from adding a single AI feature to building a full agentic AI system. I build AI agents that can reason, plan, and use tools to complete multi-step tasks autonomously — using frameworks like LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, and AutoGen. This includes tool-calling and function-calling agents, multi-agent orchestration, ReAct and chain-of-thought reasoning, memory systems, and human-in-the-loop guardrails.

If you need to build an AI agent, integrate ChatGPT (OpenAI API), Claude (Anthropic API), or Gemini into your product, or ship an autonomous workflow, these are core specialties. I currently lead AI development on a production agentic AI SaaS platform.

LLM development, RAG systems & custom chatbots

LLM application development turns large language models into reliable product features. With RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation), I connect your documents, databases, and knowledge to an LLM so answers are accurate, grounded, and cited — reducing hallucinations. This uses vector databases (Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, ChromaDB, pgvector), embeddings, hybrid search, reranking, and prompt engineering with evaluation.

Common builds: a RAG chatbot for customer support, an internal knowledge assistant, a document Q&A tool, or a voice AI agent. Chatbots can live on your website, WhatsApp, or inside your app.

RAG development custom chatbot development vector database developer LLM integration expert

Generative AI services add creative and media capabilities to your product: text-to-image generation (ComfyUI, Replicate, FAL.ai), speech-to-text and text-to-speech (TTS), real-time transcription, OCR, face swap, and video/audio AI.
