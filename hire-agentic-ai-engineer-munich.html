Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from HR managers and business owners looking to hire AI engineers

What is the difference between Generative AI and Agentic AI development?
Generative AI development focuses on integrating models like GPT-4 or Stable Diffusion to generate text, images, or audio. Agentic AI development goes further — building autonomous systems that use AI to make decisions, call external APIs, execute multi-step tasks, and operate independently. I specialize in both.
Can you integrate AI into my existing software product?
Yes. I can add AI capabilities to any existing web application — whether it's adding a chatbot, building a RAG system for document Q&A, generating content automatically, or creating an AI-powered recommendation engine. I will assess your current stack and integrate AI cleanly without disrupting your existing system.
What LLMs do you have experience with?
I have production experience with OpenAI GPT-4/GPT-4o, DeepSeek, Anthropic Claude, Hugging Face models, Ollama (local inference), and various specialized models through Replicate. I design multi-model routing systems that select the best model for each task while optimizing cost.
How do you handle AI cost and rate limit management?
I implement token budgeting, response caching, model fallback chains, async queuing with Celery, and usage tracking per user/organization. These strategies reduce AI API costs significantly while maintaining performance and reliability in production.
